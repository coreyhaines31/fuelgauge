Claude Max usage limits

Max is one plan now: $100 a month for 5x Pro's usage, every five hours and every week. The old Max 20x is called Ultra.

The short answer

Claude Max costs $100 a month and gives five times the Pro plan's usage every five hours and every week. It has a 5-hour session limit, a weekly limit across all models, and a separate Fable cap: up to 50% of the weekly limit can go to Fable models. The weekly limit resets at a fixed time assigned to your account. The plan once called Max 20x is now Ultra.

What 5x means

Max gives five times what Pro allows, in both windows: each five-hour session and each week. It doesn't say how many messages that is, because Anthropic doesn't publish a count for any plan.

The weekly limit is the one heavy Claude Code users run into. It applies across all models and resets at a fixed time assigned to your account, not on a calendar day everyone shares. Settings › Usage on claude.ai shows when yours resets.

The Fable cap

Fable models are part of Max. You can spend up to 50% of your weekly limit on them, and they use it faster than other models. That's a cap inside the weekly limit, not extra room. At the cap, switch to another model to stay within your plan, or keep using Fable on usage credits. See the Claude weekly limit for how the two fit together.

What happened to Max 20x?

Max 20x is now the Ultra plan, at the same $200 a month. Subscribers moved over automatically. Ultra gives 10x Pro's usage every week, and verified Ultra accounts have no 5-hour limit. Claude usage limits compares every plan.

What happens when you hit it

Wait for the reset, turn on usage credits, or upgrade to Ultra, which applies right away. Usage credits also let you keep using Fable past its cap. The $100 a month in API credits that comes with Max can't be used in Claude Code or the Claude apps.

In Claude Code, the wrap-up allowance lets the current step finish when you hit the 5-hour limit mid-response. On Max it can apply each time you reach a 5-hour limit, as long as you're inside your weekly limit.

Will you make it to the reset?

A percentage tells you how much you've used. It doesn't tell you whether, at the pace you're going, you'll run out before the window resets. Fuel Gauge does that math: a free Mac menu bar app that reads your Claude Code limits after every reply and says "Makes it to reset" or "Runs out around 3:40p, 1h 20m early". It can put the same line under your Claude Code prompt.

Questions

Is there still a Claude Max 20x plan?

No. Max 20x is now called Ultra, at $200 a month. Existing Max 20x subscribers moved to Ultra automatically with no price change.

Does Claude Max have a weekly limit?

Yes. A weekly limit applies across all models and resets at a fixed time assigned to your account. Max also caps Fable at up to 50% of the weekly limit.

When does the Claude Max weekly limit reset?

At a fixed time assigned to your account, once a week. Settings › Usage on claude.ai shows your next reset.

Can I use Max's API credits in Claude Code?

No. The monthly API credits included with Max can't be used in Claude Code or the Claude apps, including for usage credits.
