Claude Pro vs Max

Same features, same kinds of limits. Max buys five times Pro's usage. Ultra, the plan that used to be Max 20x, buys ten times Pro's week and drops the 5-hour limit.

The short answer

Pro is $20 a month. Max is $100 and gives five times Pro's usage every five hours and every week, with Fable included up to half the weekly limit. Ultra, formerly Max 20x, is $200 and gives ten times Pro's weekly usage, with no 5-hour limit once your account is verified. All of them share their limits with Claude Code. Upgrade when you hit your limits often enough that waiting costs you more than the difference.

How to tell if you need Max

Anthropic doesn't publish message counts, so you can't pick a plan from a spec sheet. Your own usage is the better guide:

  • You hit the 5-hour limit most working days. Max gives five times the room. That's the clearest signal.
  • You want Fable in your plan. On Pro it runs on usage credits; Max and Ultra include it, up to half the week.
  • You run Claude Code for hours, with subagents or loops. Long agentic sessions use plans up fastest. If even Max's week runs out, Ultra doubles it and drops the 5-hour limit.
  • You rarely see a limit warning. Stay on Pro, and try the habits in how to stop hitting Claude limits first.

Upgrading applies right away. Usage credits are the in-between option: keep your plan and pay for extra usage only when you go over.

To check, open Settings › Usage or run /usage in Claude Code.

Let your pace decide

The question isn't how much you used today. It's whether your usual pace makes it to the reset. Fuel Gauge tracks that for every window and tells you, in the menu bar and under your Claude Code prompt, whether you'll make it. A week of "runs out early" on Pro is a good reason to try Max. A week of "makes it, about 60% by then" means Pro is enough.

Questions

Is Claude Max worth it over Pro?

If you hit Pro's 5-hour or weekly limit most days, usually yes: Max gives five times Pro's usage every five hours and every week for $100 a month, and includes Fable. If you rarely hit a limit, Pro is enough.

What happened to Claude Max 20x?

It's now the Ultra plan, at the same $200 a month. Ultra gives 10x Pro's weekly usage and has no 5-hour limit on verified accounts.

Do Pro, Max, and Ultra have the same weekly reset?

Each has a weekly limit that resets at a fixed time assigned to your account. Settings › Usage shows yours.
